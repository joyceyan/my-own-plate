Privacy Policy for MyOwnPlate

Effective date: August 13, 2026

1. Data we collect

MyOwnPlate stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Profile information you enter during onboarding (name, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and goals).
  • Photos of meals you choose to log.
  • Nutrition estimates and meal timestamps you save in the app.

2. How we use your data

All data is used only inside the app to display your daily nutrition totals, generate estimated calorie and macro recommendations based on your profile, and keep a history of meals you log.

3. Where your data is stored

MyOwnPlate does not send your personal data, meal photos, or nutrition history to our servers. Everything is stored locally on your iPhone. The AI model that analyzes food photos is included in the app and runs entirely on-device.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete the app. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data, including meal photos and model files.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. The onboarding flow requires users to be at least 13 years old.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date and notify you in the app.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@myownplate.app.
